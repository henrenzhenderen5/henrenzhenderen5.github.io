I am **Tian Tianle**, a Robotics student at the National University of Singapore (NUS). I hold a bachelor's degree in Electronic Information from Fudan University, where I graduated in 2026. I am interested in embedded systems, robotics, electronic instrumentation, signal processing, and the practical integration of hardware and software.

My work combines electronics, programming, and hands-on prototyping. I enjoy taking an idea from circuit-level design through assembly, debugging, and a working physical system. My current technical toolkit includes C, Python, Verilog, MATLAB, Vivado, Altium Designer, JLCEDA, and SOLIDWORKS.

This website presents selected projects in embedded systems and electronic design, including an autonomous tic-tac-toe robot and an award-winning superheterodyne spectrum analyzer.

## Selected Projects

### [Autonomous Tic-Tac-Toe Robot](/portfolio/tic-tac-toe-robot/)

A vision-guided embedded game device that uses an OpenMV camera, MicroPython, and inter-module communication to recognize a physical tic-tac-toe board and execute moves automatically.

### [Superheterodyne Spectrum Analyzer](/portfolio/superheterodyne-spectrum-analyzer/)

An award-winning electronic-instrument project based on a superheterodyne architecture, developed for the 2025 Fudan University Electronic Design Competition. The project received First Prize and ranked first in its competition category.
