I am **Tian Tianle**, an undergraduate student in Electronic Information at Fudan University. I am interested in embedded systems, electronic instrumentation, signal processing, and the practical integration of hardware and software.

My work combines electronics, programming, and hands-on prototyping. I enjoy taking an idea from circuit-level design through assembly, debugging, and a working physical system. My current technical toolkit includes C, Python, Verilog, MATLAB, Vivado, Altium Designer, JLCEDA, and SOLIDWORKS.

This portfolio presents selected projects in embedded systems and electronic design, including an autonomous tic-tac-toe robot and an award-winning superheterodyne spectrum analyzer.
