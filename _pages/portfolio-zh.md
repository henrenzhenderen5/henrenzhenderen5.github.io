## [视觉引导的井字棋机械臂]({{ '/portfolio-zh/tic-tac-toe-robot/' | relative_url }})

[![视觉引导的井字棋机械臂]({{ '/images/portfolio/tic-tac-toe-cover.jpg' | relative_url }})]({{ '/portfolio-zh/tic-tac-toe-robot/' | relative_url }})

用 OpenMV 识别实体棋盘和黑白棋子，再由机械臂从棋盘外取棋并放到目标格。项目涵盖嵌入式视觉、串口通信、舵机控制与整机联调。

## [超外差频谱分析仪]({{ '/portfolio-zh/superheterodyne-spectrum-analyzer/' | relative_url }})

[![超外差频谱分析仪]({{ '/images/portfolio/spectrum-analyzer-cover.jpg' | relative_url }})]({{ '/portfolio-zh/superheterodyne-spectrum-analyzer/' | relative_url }})
