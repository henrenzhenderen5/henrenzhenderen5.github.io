## Overview

**Superheterodyne Spectrum Analyzer** is an electronic-instrument project developed by a three-person team for the 2025 Fudan University Electronic Design Competition. The project applies a superheterodyne architecture to frequency-spectrum observation and analysis, translating a high-frequency input signal into a form suitable for controlled processing and display.

The project received **First Prize** and ranked **first in its competition category**.

![Superheterodyne Spectrum Analyzer](/images/portfolio/spectrum-analyzer-cover.jpg)

## My Contribution

As a core team member, I collaborated on the design, implementation, testing, and refinement of the instrument. The project required us to turn a receiver-architecture concept into a functioning prototype within a competition setting, while coordinating closely across circuit design, integration, measurement, and debugging.

## Design Concept

The analyzer is based on the superheterodyne principle. Instead of processing the input signal only at its original frequency, the system mixes it with a local oscillator to generate an intermediate-frequency signal. This architecture makes subsequent filtering, amplification, and analysis more controllable, forming the basis for spectrum observation.
