## Overview

**Autonomous Tic-Tac-Toe Robot** is a compact embedded game device that combines machine vision, hardware integration, and motion control to play tic-tac-toe automatically. Developed during an intensive one-month project, it translates the state of a physical game board into machine-readable information and carries out the next move through a robotic mechanism.

![Autonomous Tic-Tac-Toe Robot](/images/portfolio/tic-tac-toe-cover.jpg)

## My Contribution

As a core team member, I contributed to the embedded-system design and hands-on hardware implementation. My work included:

- Programming an OpenMV camera in MicroPython for visual recognition of the tic-tac-toe board.
- Applying I2C and related communication protocols to connect the embedded modules.
- Supporting the integration, assembly, and soldering of the circuit board.
- Debugging the system so that vision input, game logic, and physical execution could work together reliably.

## Technical Approach

The OpenMV camera observes the board and identifies the current game state. The embedded controller then uses this information to determine and perform the next action. Building the prototype required close coordination between software and hardware: camera programming, inter-module communication, PCB assembly, and practical debugging were treated as one integrated workflow.

## Tools

OpenMV, MicroPython, Solidworks, I2C, embedded systems, PCB assembly, and soldering.
